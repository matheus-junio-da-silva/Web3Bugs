    uint256 _voteStart = proposalSnapshot(pId);
    uint256 _voteEnd = proposalDeadline(pId);

    assert _voteEnd == voteEnd_, "End date was changed";

## Predicado e condição de falha
As assertions textuais dentro deste intervalo são: `_voteStart == voteStart_, "Start date was changed"` e `_voteEnd == voteEnd_, "End date was changed"`. A refutação exigiria um caminho executável, admitido pelos `require`, em que ao menos uma seja falsa.
- Predicado da matriz semântica: `_voteStart == voteStart_, "Start date was changed" || _voteEnd == voteEnd_, "End date was changed"`.
- Sequência de chamadas: `proposalSnapshot; proposalDeadline; proposalCreated; f`. Preservar ordem e semântica de revert, inclusive `lastReverted`.
