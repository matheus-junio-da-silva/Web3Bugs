**No-execution versus inapplicability:** The `N/A` rows for #24, #25 and #26 above are the only established nonapplicable combinations from the PDF. The existing indexed IDs for #25/#26 even where report says N/A represent **counterfactual source/target interpretations, not claims of report-era executed checks**; these require a future catalog-policy decision on removal versus retaining a clearly marked nonapplicable record. For #27–32 the report itself calls the properties handler-applicable; absence of a separate example is **a documented coverage gap**, never attributed to inapplicability.

**Dependency blockers and historical identity:** Eight exact 2021 implementations are absent: `HAaveProtocol`, `HOneInchExchange`, `HBalancer`, `HGasTokens`, `HKyberNetwork`, `HMooniswap`, `HUniswap`, `HWeth`. Fifteen modern same-named implementations are *nominally present* but not proven equivalent to the audited commit `c90dec1046e5e26edf70f985dd86381403912482`. The driver-injected Solidity `stack_slot` and inherited `_getCubeCounter()` are absent from the later implementation; `CUBE_COUNTER_KEY()` also has no matching exposed function. `ProxyHarness` constructor and internal override signatures differ from `Proxy.sol`. The historical Prover used bounded unrolling and simplified unsafe assembly, with external DeFi behavior assumed rather than verified.

**Outcome rule:** all handler-specific #22/#23/#25/#26 records are `Result=NOT_STATED`/`Detection Outcome=NOT_STATED` unless an individual formal outcome and context are independently established. Handler cell `None`, `Expected`, `N/A` and `🔎` have distinct interpretations. `tokenMovementCorrectness` preserves the literal generated-token pre-balance read without `at initialStorage`; it does not demonstrate token conservation. `noOverwrite` asserts flat storage behavior, not the historical High local-memory safety issue. The separate 2021 reentrancy finding cannot silently turn current modern-source proof status into a reproduced `VIOLATED`.

**Release gate:** PR remains **draft** until these target-specific applications are resolved or explicitly waived by independent review. The matrix is an evidence ledger, not a declaration of semantic completeness.
