## Main issues relevant to interpretation (PDF pages 1–3)

<a id="stable-additive-mint"></a>
**Medium, stable debt additive mint:** The report's issue table expressly names `Additive mint (Stable debt token)` and attributes an error to rounding during conversions at high conversion rates. It states that the mitigation avoids transfer on mint/burn of zero stable debt tokens. This is a real historic report finding, independent of any assignment to a particular supplied CVL file. Item #14 in the formal-property section is marked `✔*`, while the exact preserved `additiveMint` rule assumes zero stable rate and operates with an interest-free harness. The report does not show that this exact constrained rule produced or directly supported the issue; for example 6 Detection Outcome is `NOT_STATED`, with the historical finding separately retained as AV2-01 in `results/bugs.csv`.

Other reported issues include an incorrect DebtToken totalSupply due to missing interest accumulation, AToken burn rounding, user-debt and balance growth under interest redirection, interest-related division by zero, liquidation health factor, and collateral withdrawal. The report does not establish an exact source-rule-to-token match for every such issue. No automatic finding attribution is made to other Project 12 source instances.

### High-level equations and context (paraphrased, not substituted for literal PDF)

- Supply integrity is a relation between reported total supply and the aggregate over all account balances. Report #10 is discussed as a consequence of #11 and #12; report #18 analogously refers to #19 and #20. These implications do not give each narrower CVL source rule an independently reported status.
- Stable and variable mint/burn rules express a post-state balance delta or equivalence between split and combined operations. The report's ✔* qualifier explicitly **removes rounding from the verified code**; the literal CVL code must be used to determine its own exact signed/unsigned arithmetic.
- UserConfiguration #26 checks four implications/equivalences between bitmap-derived getters; #27–#30 verify setter correctness and preservation of other reserve slots. The report includes a user argument in its mathematical interface while `UserConfigurationHarness` stores one `usersConfig` map, so its source instantiation must not be extrapolated to multi-user isolation.
- AToken transfer/mint/burn properties admit a tolerance `epsilon`, constrained by `epsilon <= normalizedIncome()/ray` in the notation section. The limit is not a guarantee about the Source StableDebtToken or VariableDebtToken verification outside ✔* conditions.
- The LendingPool discussion uses per-user net assets formed from underlying balance plus aToken balance minus stable and variable debt balance. This is report-wide context, not a separately supplied Project 12 CVL source assertion.

### Preserved report contradictions and qualified statements

- The UserConfiguration #29 displayed postcondition names `isBorrowing`, despite the title/prose referring to collateral and the literal source asserting `isUsingAsCollateral`. No repair or cross-propagation.
- VariableDebtToken #24 is titled burn additivity, but one descriptive line calls it minting; the source actually invokes burn.
- The AToken report #8 describes burn success preconditions, but one printed mathematical bound has `balanceOf(u) < x`, which appears inconsistent with the accompanying sufficient-balance prose. This document does not silently correct the PDF.
- In the UserConfiguration functions list, the prose description of `isEmpty` is inconsistent with the meaning of the name and `UserConfiguration.sol:108-109` (`self.data == 0`); the CVL getter assertions use the Solidity behavior.
- The medium stable-debt rounding issue is evidenced by the historical *Main Issues* table, not by an identified counterexample from the **specific** `StableDebtToken.spec:126-142` under its zero-user-rate precondition and incompatible/simplified historical harness.
- The PDF disclaimer limits guarantees to the specification actually supplied and denies that the report represents comprehensive security assurance. No current-Prover or unqualified interest/rounding proof is claimed.

## PDF versioning and numbering cautions

The report's *Aave-proposed properties* portion contains two different numbering contexts: preliminary global invariants P1–P7, followed by operation-oriented sections whose labels restart from P6 (deposit), then continue through P41 (withdraw). Do not identify the repeated P6/P7 as the same requirement or count them as separate active CVL declarations. P41 also uses the word *deposit* in a withdrawal-rate discussion; preserve this as a report wording inconsistency. The separately itemized critical-property subset near the conclusion references earlier proposal numbers, not additional proofs.

The five recorded `NOT_STATED` CVL results are deliberate: the report does verify the conceptual aggregate balance/supply relationships #12 and #20 under ✔*, but it does not identify their source-specific decomposition and exact target context in a way sufficient for rule-by-rule attribution. Reporting a proof of an aggregate does not warrant manufacturing per-rule evidence.
