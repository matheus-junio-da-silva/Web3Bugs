## Scope and historical issues

The audit covers ATokenVault and ATokenVaultV2. Certora describes defects identified on earlier code versions and fixes verified on the final reviewed implementation; violating older versions are not necessarily the latest version.

1. **Medium:** DoS after underlying deposit due to rounding difference in aToken received versus lastVaultBalance used for yield accounting; fixed PR #70 (`32edfe6`).
2. **Medium:** Same-block aToken gifts can prevent withdrawals, causing a revert when redemption withdraws assets beyond the old baseline; fixed PR #82 (`385b397`).
3. **Medium:** Insolvency from claimable fees exceeding aToken holdings, connected to report aggregates #14/#15; fixed PR #82.
4. **Medium:** Full DoS if totalAssets subtracts fees larger than held assets and underflows; connected to report aggregate #14; fixed PR #82.
5. **Low:** previewRedeem can return more assets than are actually redeemed when pending fees affect the rate; fixed PR #82.
6. **Low:** lastVaultBalance rounding differences can accumulate incorrect fee charges or losses; linked to report aggregate #13; fixed PR #86.
7. **Low:** Deposits of existing aTokens can revert because of Aave's new-supply cap despite no new supplied underlying; fixed PR #80 (`34ad6e3`).
8. **Recommendation:** reject zero owner in initialize to avoid losing admin access; fixed PR #71 (`3927afd`).
9. **Informational:** same-block front-running gift can bypass fee assessment; fixed PR #82.
10. **Informational / accepted:** ERC4626 preview methods depend on Aave caps, withdrawal liquidity or balances. Strict limit-independence checks are violated, but the behavior is considered acceptable for Aave-backed Vault.
11. **Informational / accepted:** ERC4626 nonrevert expectations may fail because of Aave arithmetic/conversion checks, which the report treats as intentional or acceptable.

## Formal qualifiers and assumptions

The report distinguishes verified latest (tick), verified under assumptions/simplifications (tick star), violated under at least one version (cross), and timeout. It reports loop unrolling that does not cover counterexamples requiring more than one iteration, excludes verification of WithSig cryptographic signature correctness, and frequently assumes `totalSupply()` and related token scaled supplies are bounded by `maxUint128()`. PreviewMint footnote #3 and previewRedeem footnote #4 assume inverse conversions at the relevant maxDeposit/maxAssetsWithdrawableFromAave bounds. Footnote #5 assumes liquidity index in [1,2] for lastVaultBalance, and footnote #6 adds index/idealized ray arithmetic for the aggregate positiveSupply check. Exact per-source ✔* or timeout markers cannot be assigned to an unnamed variant. The supplied PropertyGPT snapshot lacks original execution config and exact contracts at each earlier historical revision, so the report does not prove faithful replay from this source alone.

### Numerical and mathematical examples reproduced as formulas (PDF pp. 2–8)

**Deposit rounding, PDF p. 2.** The report's example begins with liquidity index \(10\,RAY\), share supply \(100\,RAY\) and total assets \(101\,RAY\). A deposit of 91 underlying units yields a down-rounded share amount of 90 from approximately 90.099 shares; conversion back rounds up to 91 underlying units. Only aToken worth 90 is minted by the index-scaled pool. The hypothetical outdated implementation increments \`lastVaultBalance\` by 91, creating a 192 baseline against actual reserves of 191 and making \`newVaultBalance - lastVaultBalance\` underflow.

**Withdrawal/griefing gift, PDF pp. 2–3, footnote 1.** In the unrounded analytical model, a sufficient gifted amount obeys:

\[
\mathrm{giftAmount} >
\mathrm{totalAssets}(t_0)
\left(\frac{\mathrm{totalShares}(t_0)}{\mathrm{BobSharesToRedeem}}-1\right).
\]

The report derives this from demanding that redeemable assets exceed the recorded reserve baseline, assuming the initial baseline is synchronized. One worked scenario starts with 200 assets/200 shares, adds one deposited asset/share, then receives a gift of 3: actual assets become 204 with 201 shares and \`lastVaultBalance=201\`. Redeeming 200 shares gives about 202 assets, more than the old baseline, so the outdated subtraction can revert. The report restricts this scenario to the same block; subsequent accrual can resynchronize.

**Fee insolvency, PDF pp. 4–5, footnote 2.** The approximate gift bound is:

\[
\mathrm{giftAmount}\ge
\frac{\mathrm{totalAssets}(t_0)-\mathrm{withdrawnAmount}}{\mathrm{feePercentage}},
\quad
\mathrm{attackerLoss}=\mathrm{giftAmount}
\left(1-\frac{\mathrm{withdrawnAmount}}{\mathrm{totalAssets}(t_0)}\right).
\]

The report explicitly conditions this bound on both \(\mathrm{withdrawnAmount}\le \mathrm{totalAssets}(t_0)\) and \(\mathrm{ATokenBalance}-\mathrm{withdrawnAmount}\ge\mathrm{giftAmount}\). The 5% example starts with accumulated fees 700, reserves 1,700, baseline 1,700 and share supply 1,000. A modeled gift of 620 through a withdrawal-to-vault reduces share supply and recorded baseline without normal fee collection. A later same-block withdrawal of 970 leaves reserves 730, baseline 110 and positive residual share supply. On the next block the fees formula yields \(700+0.05(730-110)=731\), greater than the actual 730 aTokens. \`totalAssets()\` can then underflow; the report describes the condition as persistent after subsequent accrual.

**Fee rounding, PDF p. 6.** Each deposit/withdrawal may change the gap between \`lastVaultBalance\` and \`AToken.balanceOf(vault)\` by about one unit; after \(k\) operations the discrepancy can be approximately \(\pm k\). Positive and negative gaps affect protocol fee collection in opposite directions.

**Preview return value, PDF pp. 5–6.** A stale same-block exchange ratio approximately 1:2.63, later corrected to approximately 1:2.55, can cause a user's \`previewRedeem()\` quote to exceed what is sent upon a later \`redeem()\`. This is an older-version cross-transaction scenario, distinct from the same-transaction rule \`previewRedeem_amount_check\`.

**New-asset supply caps, PDF p. 7.** The supply-limiting internal method returns zero for inactive/frozen/paused markets, an unrestricted maximum when there is no cap, or remaining supply capacity otherwise. These limits apply to new underlying supplied to Aave, not necessarily to wrapping aTokens already minted. The report identifies the mistaken conflation.

### Limitations, symbols and footnotes (PDF pp. 9, 12–13)

The report defines ✓ as proof on its latest tested revision; ✓* as proof using additional simplifying assumptions; ✗ as a violation in at least one tested revision; and the clock as timeout with neither proof nor counterexample. Do **not** interpret a report ✗ followed by latest ✓ as VIOLATED on the final build. The report explains that loops were unrolled and multi-iteration traces could escape detection; cryptographic verification of WithSig permits/signatures was excluded. Many rules assume \`totalSupply()\`, underlying supply and scaled aToken supply never exceed the 128-bit bound.

Footnotes are represented here with their original **numbers and mathematical conditions**:

1. The gift/redemption bound derives from requiring \`lastVaultBalance < sharesToRedeem * (initialAssets + gift)/initialShares\`, assuming the worst-case starting baseline equals reserves.
2. The fee insolvency bound is conditional on withdrawal not exceeding initial assets and the remaining aToken reserves being at least the gift amount.
3. Left-inverse assumption \(\forall x,\ \mathrm{convertToAssets}(\mathrm{convertToShares}(x))=x\), needed specifically at \(x=\mathrm{maxDeposit}()\), linked to report property #3.
4. The same left-inverse relationship needed at \(x=\mathrm{maxAssetsWithdrawableFromAave}()\), linked to property #7.
5. Liquidity index modeled within \(1\le \mathrm{index}\le 2\), linked to report property #13.
6. The index bound plus idealized ray arithmetic laws (for all \(x,\mathrm{ind},z\)), equating rayMultiply(rayDivide(x,index) ± z,index) with x ± rayMultiply(z,index), are used for some fee/positive-supply checks (#14–15). These are *assumptions*, not demonstrations that the real Aave rounding functions satisfy them globally.

### Independent semantic sanity notice (NOT stated as a historical report finding)

Original \`fees_LEQ_ATokenBal.spec\`, \`lastVaultBal_LEQ_ATokenBalThis.spec\` and \`positiveSupply_imply_positiveAssets.spec\` have a universally quantified \`rayMul_g\` axiom where \`rayMul_g(1,0)=0\` and \`1<=rayMul_g(1,0)\` both hold, which cannot be satisfied simultaneously over integers. \`accrueYieldCheck.spec\` also has a universal strict inequality that reduces to \`0<0\` at zero denominator/rounding=0. **No axiom text was modified.** Without the original verifier invocation and a successful solver sanity run, these are mathematical contradiction witnesses in the displayed CVL, not claims that Certora did or did not reject the proof. Never equate report VERIFIED with an independently reproduced proof under a satisfiable assumption model.
