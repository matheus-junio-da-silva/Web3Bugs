## Reported high-level specifications

The Accounts section covers account validity, signer/account correspondence, unique and persistent authorization, and multiple signer roles. The LockedGold section covers nonvoting balances, conservation of account holdings, cross-account isolation, unlock timing and withdrawal liveness. The Elections section covers the consistency of votes, assets and voting units. The ReleaseGold section covers withdrawal additivity, limits, timelocks, and state monotonicity. Sorted linked lists are checked for well-formedness and ordering. StableToken ERC20 verification assumes inflation rate and factor fixed at one.

## Qualifications and limits

The report explicitly scopes guarantees to the provided specifications, rather than every possible protocol behavior. The original source spells its specification language **Specify**; the supplied project uses historical CVL/Specify syntax. The report says major issues were corrected and subsequent checks verified fixes, but does not provide a per-source-rule, per-target formal result table identifying the 61 extracted active instances. Therefore the catalog should use `NOT_STATED` for individual results and audit outcomes without explicit matching evidence.

Some public project scripts apply source transformations before verification (including simplified arithmetic, proposal-pass condition, and mocked external calls); these are **not** silently treated as exact equivalence to the unmodified Solidity snapshot. See the consolidated catalog documentation.
