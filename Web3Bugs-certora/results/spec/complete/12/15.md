## Reconstructed methodology
Obtain index, save initialStorage, mint x and y sequentially, then compare to a single x+y mint evaluated at that initial storage. This traces the CVL assertion; it is not a claim about unpublished auditor reasoning.

## Why the Solidity excerpt is relevant
mint converts amount into scaled debt via rayDiv(index), _mint stores that scaled amount, and balanceOf reconstructs via rayMul(current index). Two rayDiv roundings need not equal one combined rounding. The selected `.sol` blocks show exact functions and before/after accounting state involved in the rule.
