## Motivation
Detect unrelated simultaneous balance mutations in a single operation.

## Reconstructed methodology
Record two distinct accounts' balances, invoke a symbolic method once, and assert at least one recorded balance is unchanged. This description reconstructs the observable assertion, not the auditor's private reasoning.

## Why the Solidity excerpt is relevant
Mint/burn update a user's debt while the harness substitutes principal balances for accrued balances. The condition is an OR: at least one of two distinct balances must be unchanged.
