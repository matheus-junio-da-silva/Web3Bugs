## Reconstructed methodology

## Why the Solidity excerpt is relevant
setBorrowing clears only the even bit for a reserve then writes borrowing at that bit; isBorrowing shifts and masks the corresponding bit. The `UserConfigurationHarness` wrappers expose the same storage map to the CVL calls, and `DataTypes.UserConfigurationMap` supplies its `data` field.
