## Motivation
Ensure updating a borrow flag for one reserve does not modify a different reserve's borrow/collateral bits.

## Reconstructed methodology
Read two flags for another reserve, update borrowing for the selected reserve, assert those two flag observations remain unchanged. This follows the public source code, rather than claiming private auditor thought processes.

## Why the Solidity excerpt is relevant
setBorrowing updates bit 2r only; readouts for r' inspect bits 2r' and 2r'+1. The source asserts both are preserved, not just borrow. The `UserConfigurationHarness` wrappers expose the same storage map to the CVL calls, and `DataTypes.UserConfigurationMap` supplies its `data` field.
