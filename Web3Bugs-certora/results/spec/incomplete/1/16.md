## Motivation

The property is intended to establish that a newly allocated action-set ID becomes queued and advances the counter.

## Reconstructed methodology

The following reasoning is reconstructed from the source property, available Solidity code, and the public report; it is not presented as private auditor reasoning.

1. Take the next action-set counter value as a fresh ID.
2. Establish the fresh-ID invariant.
3. Queue a new set.
4. Assert that the ID is queued and the counter advanced.
