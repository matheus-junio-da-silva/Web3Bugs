## Source/report interpretation note

The simple invariant and the extension rule should not be read as contradictory. The invariant demands the backing inequality after each preserved target call. In the supplied final Solidity snapshot, LendToAaveMigrator.initialize anticipates the later AAVE-token rescue by subtracting lendToAaveAmount in its own internal collateral check before that LEND has actually been transferred to the LEND token address. The source invariant itself does not make that anticipatory subtraction.

LendIsBackedByAaveIncInitialize separately models the migrator initializer followed immediately by AAVE_ORIG.initialize with the same lendToAaveAmount, after which the LEND-token balance used by the invariant can reflect that second step. This makes a violated simple invariant and a verified paired-sequence rule semantically coherent.

The report's violated notation means violated under one of the tested versions, not necessarily that the final supplied Solidity snapshot remains vulnerable. The catalog therefore preserves VIOLATED / FINDING without claiming that every final-state execution still violates the invariant.

## Motivation

Ensure that the AAVE reserve left in the LEND migrator is sufficient to cover circulating LEND at the configured LEND-to-AAVE conversion ratio, even as migration and rescue operations move LEND and AAVE balances.

## Reconstructed methodology

The following reasoning is reconstructed from the source property, available Solidity code, and public report; it is not presented as private auditor reasoning.

1. Read the LEND total supply through LEND1.totalSupply().
2. Read the amount of LEND held at the LEND token contract through LEND1.balanceOf(LEND1).
3. Subtract that balance from total supply and divide by LEND_AAVE_RATIO(), obtaining the AAVE amount needed to collateralize the remaining LEND according to the source assertion.
4. Read the AAVE balance of the current target contract through AAVE1.balanceOf(currentContract).
5. Require the computed backing need to be less than or equal to the AAVE balance.
6. For invariant preservation, restrict the environment so the caller is neither the modeled LEND token nor the modeled AAVE token.

A counterexample would therefore exhibit an allowed preserved execution after which (LEND total supply - LEND held by the LEND contract) / ratio is greater than the AAVE held by the migrator.

## Why the Solidity excerpt is relevant

LendToAaveMigrator stores the concrete AAVE, LEND, and LEND_AAVE_RATIO references at lines 13-15 and initializes them in the constructor at lines 40-46. Its rescue initializer computes lendAmount, transfers AAVE out of the migrator, transfers LEND to the LEND token contract, and checks a collateralization expression at lines 57-79. The final check explicitly subtracts lendToAaveAmount before division, anticipating the later AAVE-token initializer described by the report. That is precisely why this internal check is not identical to the CVL invariant, which instead observes the actual LEND1.balanceOf(LEND1) at the current state. migrateFromLEND at lines 94-104 likewise moves LEND toward the LEND-token holding/burn address while paying AAVE out at the configured ratio.
