## Motivation

Check that the migrator remains sufficiently backed not only across ordinary methods, but also across the rescue sequence in which the migrator initializer is followed by the AAVE token initializer using the same lendToAaveAmount.

## Reconstructed methodology

The following reasoning is reconstructed from the source property, available Solidity code, and public report; it is not presented as private auditor reasoning.

1. Require the caller to differ from the modeled LEND and AAVE token contracts.
2. Require the LEND-to-AAVE backing inequality to hold before the tested execution.
3. Select an arbitrary method f.
4. If f is the four-argument migrator initialize, call it with symbolic rescue amounts.
5. In that branch, bind lendToken to LEND1, create symbolic token/amount arrays, and call AAVE_ORIG.initialize with the same distributor and lendToAaveAmount.
6. Otherwise call f with arbitrary calldata.
7. Assert the same backing inequality afterward.

A counterexample would begin in a backed state and end, after one of those modeled execution paths, with insufficient AAVE backing.

This is a paired end-state proof, not a proof of every intermediate state. In the initialize branch the two initializers are executed back-to-back in the CVL rule. It therefore does not by itself establish that the simple backing invariant holds after the migrator initializer but before the AAVE initializer, nor does it cover arbitrary interleavings between those two steps. That distinction is material because the report describes the real upgrades as asynchronous and the simpler LendIsBackedByAave property is separately marked violated.

## Why the Solidity excerpt is relevant

The migrator initializer at lines 57-79 transfers AAVE out, transfers LEND to the LEND token contract, and performs its collateralization check using lendToAaveAmount. migrateFromLEND at lines 94-104 is an ordinary target method covered by the arbitrary-method branch. In AaveTokenV2.sol, safeTransfer at lines 728-734 dispatches to the token's transfer selector, and AaveTokenV2.initialize at lines 1171-1181 sends exactly lendToAaveAmount of lendToken to that same token address. That second transfer is what can increase the LEND1.balanceOf(LEND1) term observed by the final assertion. The _beforeTokenTransfer excerpt at lines 1236-1266 shows the concrete onTransfer hook call corresponding to the source summary and report assumption.
