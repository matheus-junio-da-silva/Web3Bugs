## Motivation

Ensure every outstanding stkAAVE unit remains backed by at least one unit of the underlying staked AAVE held by the staking contract, including during the rescue initializer.

## Reconstructed methodology

The following reasoning is reconstructed from the source property, available Solidity code, and public report; it is not presented as private auditor reasoning.

1. Read the staking token's totalSupply().
2. Read the modeled underlying token balance at currentContract.
3. Assert that total stkAAVE supply is less than or equal to that underlying-token balance.
4. For invariant preservation, exclude executions initiated by the modeled underlying token or by the target itself.
5. Also require that the rewards-vault address, read in a symbolic environment, is not the current target.

A counterexample would show an allowed state or preserved execution in which stkAAVE total supply exceeds the AAVE balance backing it.

## Why the Solidity excerpt is relevant

The inherited ERC20 getter at lines 489-491 returns the exact totalSupply() observed by the invariant, and _mint/_burn at lines 649-678 are the concrete updates to that liability. StakedTokenV2Rev4 exposes STAKED_TOKEN, REWARD_TOKEN, and REWARDS_VAULT at lines 1457-1465 and initializes them in the constructor. Most importantly, initialize at lines 1524-1534 can transfer listed tokens out to the rescue distributor and then enforces totalSupply() <= STAKED_TOKEN.balanceOf(address(this)); that final check is the mitigation described in the report. stake mints stkAAVE while pulling underlying in, and redeem burns stkAAVE while sending underlying out. claimRewards at lines 1601-1616 transfers REWARD_TOKEN from REWARDS_VAULT; this is the concrete use of the address constrained by REWARDS_VAULT(e2) != currentContract. Finally, _beforeTokenTransfer at lines 1799-1843 shows the aaveGovernance.onTransfer hook that corresponds to the source summary and the report's hook assumption.
