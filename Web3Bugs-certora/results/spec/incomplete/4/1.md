## Motivation

Verify that staking mints the expected number of shares and moves exactly the staked underlying amount from the depositor into the vault.

## Reconstructed methodology

The following is reconstructed from the observable CVL property, available Solidity/harness code, and report. It is not a claim about private auditor reasoning.

1. Snapshot the depositor and vault balances of the staked token and the beneficiary share balance.
2. Require bounded inputs and a zero pre-existing cooldown for the beneficiary.
3. Call stake(onBehalfOf, amount).
4. Read the post-state balances and the exchange rate **after** `stake`, not before it.
5. Assert the beneficiary share increase equals amount * exchangeRate / EXCHANGE_RATE_FACTOR and that underlying balances move by exactly amount.

A counterexample would satisfy the rule/invariant preconditions but exhibit this failure condition: A successful stake mints an unexpected share amount or transfers an underlying amount different from the requested stake amount.

## Why the Solidity excerpt is relevant

`StakedTokenV3.previewStake` and `_stake` implement share conversion, `STAKED_TOKEN.safeTransferFrom` moves underlying, and `_mint` changes the beneficiary share balance. `DummyERC20Impl` supplies the linked token balance/transfer behavior. The CVL uses **post-call** `getExchangeRate()` in the expected share delta. The supplied `_stake` does not itself change the exchange rate, but the formula alone is not a relational comparison against a recorded **pre-call** rate.
