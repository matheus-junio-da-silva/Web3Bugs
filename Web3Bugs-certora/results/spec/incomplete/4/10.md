## Motivation

Bound the shares sold during an unstake-window redeem by the amount captured when cooldown began.

## Reconstructed methodology

The following is reconstructed from the observable CVL property, available Solidity/harness code, and report. It is not a claim about private auditor reasoning.

1. Require the cooldown/balance invariants.
2. Read cooldown timestamp, cooldown share amount, and current share balance.
3. Require execution to be inside the unstake window.
4. Call redeem.
5. Compute soldShares from the target balance delta.
6. Assert soldShares equals the requested amount up to the cooldown cap.

A counterexample would satisfy the rule/invariant preconditions but exhibit this failure condition: A user redeems a number of shares inconsistent with the cooldown snapshot cap.
