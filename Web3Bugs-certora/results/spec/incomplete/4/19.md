## Motivation

Check the preview-to-received-underlying equality only for successful redemptions whose **post-call** cooldown amount still satisfies the historical rule's guard. This is a partial detector, not a general proof of redemption accounting.

## Reconstructed methodology

The following is reconstructed from the observable CVL property, available Solidity/harness code, and report. It is not a claim about private auditor reasoning.

1. Require the caller's share balance to cover `amount` and the recipient to differ from `currentContract`.
2. Compute `previewRedeem(amount)` and snapshot the recipient's underlying-token balance **before** redemption.
3. Execute `redeem`; the supplied `_redeem` may cap shares at `maxRedeemable` and decreases or deletes `stakersCooldowns[from]`.
4. Read the recipient balance and evaluate `cooldownAmount(e.msg.sender)` in the **post-call state**.
5. Only if `amount <= cooldownAmount_after(sender)`, assert that the received underlying equals the pre-call preview.

A counterexample therefore requires a successful redeem, a **true post-call antecedent**, and an underlying-token delta different from the pre-call preview. A mismatch with a false antecedent does not violate this assertion. This explicitly narrows the security behavior tested relative to the rule name, description, and report formula.

## Why the Solidity excerpt is relevant

`_redeem` computes `underlyingToRedeem` from `previewRedeem(amountToRedeem)`, burns shares, transfers STAKED_TOKEN, and **updates or deletes `stakersCooldowns[from]`** (StakedTokenV3.sol:479-527). The harness's `cooldownAmount` reads that mutable mapping (StakedAaveV3Harness.sol:27-29). For illustration, if an ordinary successful redemption starts with 100 cooled-down shares and redeems 60, the remaining cooldown is 40: the historical `60 <= cooldownAmount_after` antecedent is false even though 60 was within the **original** 100-share cooldown. If it redeems 40 instead, the remaining 60 would satisfy the guard. These examples explain the assertion's restricted coverage; they do not assert a verified counterexample or a historical execution trace.
