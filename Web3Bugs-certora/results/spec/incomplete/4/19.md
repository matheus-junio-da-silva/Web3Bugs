## Motivation

Check that previewRedeem predicts the underlying-token delta produced by redeem when the requested shares do not exceed the cooldown amount.

## Reconstructed methodology

The following is reconstructed from the observable CVL property, available Solidity/harness code, and report. It is not a claim about private auditor reasoning.

1. Require the caller share balance covers amount and the receiver is not currentContract.
2. Compute previewRedeem(amount) and snapshot receiver underlying balance.
3. Call redeem.
4. Read receiver underlying balance.
5. When amount<=cooldownAmount(sender), assert the balance delta equals the preview.

A counterexample would satisfy the rule/invariant preconditions but exhibit this failure condition: For a redeem within the cooldown amount, actual underlying received differs from previewRedeem(amount).
