## Reconstructed methodology

The following is reconstructed from the observable CVL, available Solidity, report, and recovered public verification metadata; it does not claim access to private auditor reasoning.

1. Snapshot aToken/static balances. 2. Deposit. 3. Observe whether static balance increased. 4. Start and execute cancellation. 5. Assert that an observed static mint implies no later aToken balance change.

A counterexample satisfying the rule/invariant preconditions would be: staticBalance2 > staticBalance1 but ATokenBalance3 != ATokenBalance2 after the cancellation sequence.
