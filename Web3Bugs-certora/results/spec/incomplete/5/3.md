## Reconstructed methodology

The following is reconstructed from the observable CVL, available Solidity, report, and recovered public verification metadata; it does not claim access to private auditor reasoning.

1. Choose a linked token trio and user. 2. Snapshot underlying/aToken/static balances. 3. Deposit and capture staticAmount. 4. Withdraw that staticAmount. 5. Assert static restoration unconditionally and the underlying/aToken conditions only when fromUA equals toUA.

A counterexample satisfying the rule/invariant preconditions would be: balanceS3 differs from balanceS1, or—when the deposit/withdraw token-choice flags match—the underlying delta exceeds (indexL1/RAY()+1)/2 or the aToken balance differs from its initial value.
